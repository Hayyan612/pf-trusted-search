# Audit: Princess Tower, Dubai Marina

Captured from propertyfinder.ae on 2026-11-25: **242 live ads** (144 rent, 98 sale). Every ad went through the same checks by script (`scripts/build_data.py`). Photo matches were then checked by eye (`evidence/`).

Only what a person searching can see was used: price, size, bedrooms, photos, the ad text, the agent and agency, the listing date and badges.

**Photos from another building.** The first 3 photos of every Princess Tower ad were compared with 343 ads from 8 neighbouring Dubai Marina towers (23 Marina, Elite Residence, Marina 101, The Torch, Marina Pinnacle, Sulafa Tower, Marina Crown, Ocean Heights). **No match.** In this tower, misleading photos come from other flats in the same building, not from other buildings. The ad texts were also searched for other tower names: only agency office addresses and trivia came up.

**A different person to contact.** 5 ads tell you to contact someone other than the agent the listing is under (for example a listing under Sam McCone that says to contact Umair Masood, and one under Sadaf Javaid that gives another person's mobile number in the text). Nicknames of the same person were excluded.

All 6 ads marked likely misleading carry Property Finder's Verified badge.
